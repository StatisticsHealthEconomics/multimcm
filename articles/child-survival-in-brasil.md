# Child Survival in Brasil

``` r

library(multimcm)
```

We duplicate an analysis in Lai and Yau (2009) which originated in
Sastry (1997) of A frequentist nested frailty model for survival data,
with an application to the study of child Survival in northeast Brazil
in 1976-86.

## References

Lai, Xin, and Kelvin K. W. Yau. 2009. “Multilevel Mixture Cure Models
with Random Effects.” *Biometrical Journal* 51 (3): 456–66.
<https://doi.org/10.1002/bimj.200800222>.

Sastry, Narayan. 1997. “A Nested Frailty Model for Survival Data, with
an Application to the Study of Child Survival in Northeast Brazil.”
*Journal of the American Statistical Association* 92 (438): 426–35.
<https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1997.10473994>.
